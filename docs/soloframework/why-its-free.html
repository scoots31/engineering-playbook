Why it's Free

Because price shouldn't be one more reason not to try.

Both the framework and Solo Companion are free. No catch, no upsell waiting further in.

Here's the actual reason: a lot of people never even try building something with AI, because the jargon alone makes it feel like it's not for them. That's the real barrier — not skill, not money. This exists to remove it.

If price is one more reason someone talks themselves out of trying, that's one more person who never finds out what they could've built.

This is here so anyone — whatever their background, whatever they're starting with — gets a real shot at building that confidence.
